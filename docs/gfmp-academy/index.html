Otto AI assistant

Otto is DonorDock's built-in AI assistant, trained on fundraising best practices and thousands of hours of nonprofit stewardship expertise. The DonorDock philosophy: AI should be a multiplier of human connection, never a replacement.

What Otto does:

  • Surfaces who needs attention. Identifies donor opportunities, flags lapsed givers, and recommends the next best action based on real activity patterns.
  • Sends Smart Nudges to the Action Board. Timely prompts for gift anniversaries, significant gift increases, birthdays, first-time donors, and more.
  • Triggers tasks from donor behavior. When a donor gives, lapses, or engages, Otto assigns the right task to the right person automatically.
  • Updates contact records behind the scenes. Forget manual data entry, Otto keeps records clean and current based on activity.
  • Helps with first drafts on demand. When a fundraiser asks, Otto can draft personalized emails, thank-yous, and stewardship messages to customize and send.

DonorDock works best for growing nonprofits that are ready to invest in fundraising as a system. Most commonly, that's organizations with 5+ employees, $1M+ in annual revenue, 5,000+ contacts, and a fundraising team of three or more. The common thread is readiness: a team that's outgrowing duct-tape tools and ready to commit to consistent stewardship.

What does $500/month actually include?

Everything. CRM, email, text, online giving, Otto AI, reporting, automations, integrations, project management, unlimited contacts. No feature gates. Five users included; additional users at per-user pricing.

How does DonorDock compare to Bloomerang?

Both are good platforms. The difference: Bloomerang focuses on retention analytics ("how you're doing"). DonorDock focuses on Smart Stewardship and daily action ("what to do next"). Bloomerang is modular; DonorDock is one unified plan.

How does DonorDock compare to DonorPerfect?

DonorPerfect has deep nonprofit roots. DonorDock offers a more modern interface, visual reporting (no formulas), and built-in email and giving. The "our team doesn't actually use it" complaint is the most common reason teams switch.

How long does setup take?

Most teams are up and running in days, not months. DonorDock is rated #1 Easiest Setup on G2, with implementation roughly 3.5x faster than the industry average.

How DonorDock fits with GoFundMe Pro

DonorDock and GoFundMe Pro partner on two fronts.

1. Native tech integration

DonorDock has a native, hourly-syncing integration with GoFundMe Pro. Once connected, GoFundMe Pro contacts, gifts, and event attendees flow into DonorDock automatically and become part of each donor's complete profile, where DonorDock's full toolset (Action Board, Smart Nudges, Otto, segmentation, automations, reporting) takes over.

How the connection works

  1. In GoFundMe Pro, generate API credentials (Client ID, Client Secret, Tenant/Org ID).
  2. In DonorDock, go to Settings → Integrations → GoFundMe Pro and paste in the three IDs.
  3. Toggle on, decide whether to sync event attendees, save, and test the connection.
  4. The integration syncs every hour at 5 minutes past the hour.
  5. Communication preferences sync between systems by default (configurable).

CRM glossary for fundraisers

CRM
Constituent Relationship Management. The system of record for everyone connected to the organization, donors, prospects, volunteers, board members.
Donor management platform
A CRM purpose-built for nonprofit fundraising, with giving, stewardship, and reporting baked in.
Stewardship
The ongoing work of thanking, updating, and connecting with donors so they stay engaged and keep giving.
Smart Stewardship
DonorDock's approach to stewardship: using daily action, Smart Nudges, and the Smart Steward Method to make consistent donor connection the default, not the exception.
Smart Steward Method™
DonorDock's three-step fundraising framework: Evaluate (audit your data and donor drop-off), Establish (define metrics, map journeys, automate tasks), Execute (run the system and let automation handle routine work).
Contact
A person or organization in the database. Can be an individual, a household, or an organization.
Gift
A donation. Tracked with received amount, processing fee, non-deductible portion, soft credit, and net.
Pledge
A future commitment to give, with a payment schedule.
Soft credit
Recognition for a gift the donor influenced but didn't write the check for (matching, DAF, spouse).
Recurring gift
A donation that repeats on a schedule (monthly, quarterly, annually).
Tribute gift
A donation made in honor or memory of someone.
Campaign
What the org is fundraising for (annual fund, gala, capital campaign).
Appeal
How the donation was solicited (email, direct mail, event invite).
Fund
Where the money is allocated for accounting (general fund, building fund, scholarship).
Lapsed donor
A donor who hasn't given in a defined window (DonorDock's default is 24 months).
Retention rate
The percentage of last year's donors who gave again this year.
Action Board
DonorDock's daily dashboard of prioritized fundraiser tasks.
Smart Nudges
Automated prompts triggered by donor patterns (anniversaries, lapsing, birthdays, first-time donors).
Otto
DonorDock's built-in AI assistant. Surfaces opportunities, triggers tasks, and helps fundraisers do more with less.
DAF
Donor-Advised Fund. DonorDock supports DAF gifts via Chariot integration.
