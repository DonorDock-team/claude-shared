## CRM & Donor Management

### Contact Records
Every donor gets a complete profile: personal info, giving history, communication log, engagement timeline, custom fields, relationships, and notes. Supports individuals, households, and organizations.

**Key for sales:** "One screen shows you everything about a donor — no switching between tabs or tools."

### Contact Stages
Contacts are automatically classified: Prospect, Donor, or Lapsed (based on 24-month giving window). This helps teams prioritize outreach.

### Relationship Managers (Owners)
Assign contacts to specific team members for accountability. Know who owns which relationships.

### Custom Fields
Create unlimited custom fields on contacts to track anything specific to the organization (volunteer status, event attendance, board membership, etc.).

### Badges & Segmentation
Tag contacts with badges and organize into marketing lists for targeted outreach. Filter by any combination of fields.

### Households
Group family members together to see combined giving and coordinate communications.

## ActionBoard & Smart Nudges

### ActionBoard
A daily dashboard that surfaces prioritized tasks: follow-ups to make, thank-yous to send, lapsed donors to re-engage, upcoming deadlines. Opens each morning and tells you what to do today.

**Key for sales:** This is often the "aha moment" in demos. It's the difference between a passive database and an active assistant.

### Smart Nudges
Automated prompts triggered by donor behavior patterns: giving anniversaries, lapsed giving, engagement milestones, missed follow-ups. The system watches patterns you can't track manually and alerts you at the right time.

### AskBoard (Revenue Pipeline)
Visual pipeline boards for tracking asks, proposals, and major gift opportunities through customizable stages. See your entire fundraising pipeline at a glance.

### ProjectBoard
Built-in task and project management. Assign to-dos, set deadlines, collaborate with your team — all inside the same platform.

## Online Giving

Customizable donation pages built right into the platform.

- Custom amounts and preset giving levels
- One-time and recurring donations
- Apple Pay / Google Pay support
- DAFpay (Donor-Advised Fund giving via Chariot)
- Campaign-specific giving pages
- QR codes and text-to-donate keywords
- Website embedding

### Processing Fees
DonorDock supports donor-funded processing through Stripe Connect. This means the donor covers the processing fee, so the organization pays zero in transaction costs.

**Key for sales:** "With donor-funded processing, every dollar raised goes to the mission. Compare that to competitors charging 3% on every transaction."

## Gifts & Giving Tracking

Comprehensive donation tracking:

- Multiple amount fields (received, fee, non-deductible, soft credit, net)
- Recurring gift management
- Pledge tracking with payment schedules
- Tribute gifts (honor/memorial)
- Gift tagging and categorization
- Automatic receipt generation
- Membership tracking with expiration dates

### Campaigns, Appeals & Funds
Three-dimensional gift organization:
- **Campaigns** = "what" you're fundraising for (annual fund, gala, capital campaign)
- **Appeals** = "how" donations are solicited (email appeal, direct mail, event)
- **Funds** = "where" money is directed for accounting (general fund, building fund)
