## vs. Bloomerang

### Quick comparison

| | DonorDock | Bloomerang |
|---|---|---|
| **Philosophy** | Daily action (what to do today) | Retention analytics (how you're doing) |
| **Platform** | Single unified all-in-one | Modular — CRM, fundraising, volunteer sold separately |
| **Pricing** | $500/mo, one plan, everything | ~$300/mo+ combined across modules |
| **Contacts** | Unlimited | Pricing scales with contact count |
| **AI** | Otto AI included | Penny AI assistant |
| **Smart Nudges** | Yes | No |
| **Text messaging** | Built-in | Not included |
| **Email** | Built-in drag-and-drop | Separate module |

### Framing statement
"Bloomerang does a great job with retention analytics — showing you how you're doing. DonorDock focuses on helping you take action — showing you what to do next. And because everything lives in one plan, you're not paying separately for each piece."

### When they win
Bloomerang is a reasonable choice for orgs that primarily want retention metrics and don't need text messaging or a unified platform. Their brand recognition is higher.

### When we win
When the prospect wants one system that does everything, values daily action guidance over dashboards, wants unlimited contacts, or is frustrated by paying for multiple modules.

### What NOT to say
Don't call Bloomerang "stitched together" to a prospect (even though it's true — built through acquisitions). Instead, position our unified platform as a benefit without attacking theirs.

## General Competitive Tips

1. **Ask before you pitch.** Find out what they're using today and what's frustrating them before jumping into a comparison. Their pain determines which differentiators matter.

2. **Total cost of ownership wins deals.** Most competitors look cheaper on the surface but cost more when you add modules, contact tiers, transaction fees, training costs, and setup fees.

3. **Adoption is our secret weapon.** Ask how many people on their team actually use their current CRM. Low adoption = wasted investment. DonorDock's ease of use directly solves this.

4. **Don't compete on features you don't have.** If they need peer-to-peer, enterprise customization, or deep event management — acknowledge it and help them find the right fit. Honesty builds trust even when we lose the deal.

5. **Let the guarantee do the closing.** 90-day money-back, no contracts. There's almost no risk in trying DonorDock. Lead with that when a prospect is on the fence.
