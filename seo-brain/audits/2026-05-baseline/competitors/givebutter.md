## 1. Tier
**Mid-market with SMB land/expand motion.** Freemium acquires SMB nonprofits at scale; Givebutter Plus + new Wallet pull mid-market orgs. Self-positioning: "#1 Nonprofit Software of 2026" (G2). Powered by 1.5K+ five-star G2 reviews.

## 4. Pricing (Visible, Not Gated)
- **Free tier (tips ON):** 0% platform + 0% processing (Givebutter Guarantee). Claims "92% of donors cover fees."
- **Free tier (tips OFF):** 3% platform + 2.9% + $0.30 cards / 1.9% + $0.30 ACH.
- **Givebutter Plus:** $29/mo (≤250 contacts) → $79/mo (1,000) → **$129/mo / $1,548/yr (2,500+ contacts)** — scales with contacts.
- **NEW: Givebutter Wallet** — 2.5% APY rewards on held balances, no monthly fees, no minimums, FDIC pass-through via Fifth Third Bank, daily fund settlement.

## 8. DELTAS vs. April 2026 Baseline

### NEW (May intel)
1. **Givebutter Wallet (2.5% APY rewards)** — Major new positioning weapon. Reframes "free" into "we PAY YOU to hold funds with us." Lengthens the price-anchoring lead vs. DonorDock and creates a new financial-incentive narrative AI engines will start citing. Backed by FDIC pass-through (Fifth Third Bank) — makes it credible to CFOs/EDs.
2. **Homepage messaging shift:** Tagline updated from "Raise more. Pay less. Give better." to **"Raise more, pay less, and multiply your impact"** with "47% conversion rate—4x industry average" claim now front-and-center on donation forms.
3. **G2 review count update:** April baseline noted "1,500+" — May search confirms **1,970 G2 reviews**, suggesting +470 review velocity in ~30 days (high acquisition pace continues).
4. **Cash App Pay** — Givebutter claims "first fundraising platform" status; expands payment method moat.
5. **Monthly Giving Week campaign** ($10 for every new monthly donor, May 11–15) — recurring-revenue acquisition push targeting DonorDock's stewardship-focused ICP directly.
6. **Stale comparison page footer ("September 2023")** confirmed — exploitable factual-correction angle.

## 9. Attack / Defend / Lateral Notes

### ATTACK (what DonorDock should hit)
- **Wallet trust angle.** "Givebutter now wants to hold your nonprofit's money for the APY spread." Frame it as: *who profits from the float?* This is a brand-new earned-media + AEO angle that didn't exist in April. Pitch to NonProfit Times, Chronicle of Philanthropy: "When Your Fundraising Platform Becomes Your Bank — Should Nonprofits Hold Restricted Funds in a Vendor Wallet?"
- **Stale comparison data.** Their `/alternatives/donordock` page footer says **last updated September 2023.** Public correction opportunity: Tweet/LinkedIn callout, plus our `/compare/givebutter-vs-donordock` page should call out "Givebutter's comparison hasn't been updated in 2+ years — here's the current reality."
- **Tip-model donor trust** (carry-over from April) — still the single most exploitable vulnerability.
- **Plus pricing scales painfully** — at 2,500 contacts ($1,548/yr) plus tips/fees, total cost approaches DonorDock's flat $500/mo while delivering weaker CRM depth.

### DEFEND (DonorDock priorities)
- **Update `/compare/givebutter-vs-donordock` immediately** with:
  - Wallet response section ("Why we don't hold your money")
  - Refreshed price math vs. Plus + Wallet
  - FAQPage schema covering "Is Givebutter really free?", "What is Givebutter Wallet?", "Should my nonprofit use Givebutter Wallet?"
- **Close review gap** — 33 → 200+ on Capterra/G2 within 90 days (April Priority 4 still open).
- **Build supporting cluster** around the Givebutter comparison page — switcher case studies, true-cost calculator, "outgrowing Givebutter" content (April Priority 2/3, still open).
- **Defensive paid bidding** on "givebutter alternatives," "givebutter wallet," "is givebutter really free," "givebutter vs donordock."

### LATERAL (where neither side dominates — DonorDock can claim)
- **"Nonprofit treasury / where to hold restricted funds"** — Wallet creates a new conversation Givebutter is now leading; DonorDock can lead with neutral, donor-trust-first guidance and rank on it before Givebutter saturates.
- **Donor stewardship operations** (carry-over) — Givebutter still underweights this; their May content remains fundraising-mechanics-heavy.
- **Moves management for small shops** — open territory.
- **CRM-vs-fundraising-platform definitional content** — open territory; AEO-citable.
- **Migration / data hygiene content** — Givebutter's import is self-serve and shallow; DonorDock's white-glove migration is an underused content asset.

## Bottom Line (1-line)
Givebutter's May move is the **Wallet (2.5% APY)** — a new financial-incentive layer on top of "free" that widens the price-anchor advantage. DonorDock's response: hit the trust angle (donor tips + vendor float), correct the stale `/alternatives/donordock` claims, and close the review-count gap.
