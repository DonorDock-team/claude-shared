## 1. Tier
**SMB → mid-market nonprofit.** Same ICP claim as April: "small and midsize nonprofits, lean teams." No shift up-market. NFG remains the SMB face of Bonterra's portfolio (alongside enterprise siblings EveryAction, DonorDrive, Mobilize).

## 2. Content engine size

- 9 active topical pillars: Case Management, CSR, Employee Giving, Employee Volunteering, **Fundraising**, Giving Day, Grants Management, Nonprofits, Volunteer Management.
- Estimated ~1,600–1,800 indexed URLs across blog/case studies/training/products (sitemap inventory unchanged from April baseline; sitemap fetch denied this cycle, retain April count).
- New flagship listicle **"The best nonprofit software: 31+ top solutions for 2026"** (Apr 11, 2026, ~15K words, 34 products named, **DonorDock still omitted**).

## 3. Schema coverage observed
- **NFG product page:** FAQPage (7 Qs — up from 4 in April), WebPage, Organization, BreadcrumbList. Still **no SoftwareApplication / Offer / AggregateRating / Review** markup. Schema gap unchanged.
- **Blog posts:** Article + BreadcrumbList consistent. **NEW: "Bonterra Editorial Team" byline now visible on donor-management-software guide** (was missing in April). Person schema may be attached to a generic team profile; not individual experts. Author E-E-A-T marginally improved but still weak vs. named-expert competitors.
- **No FAQPage schema on flagship 31+ listicle** despite FAQ section present — easy-win gap they still haven't closed.
- **llms.txt:** still 404. No GEO-readiness file.

## 4. Pricing observed
- **Public pricing page** (`/pricing/fundraising-and-engagement`): no dollar amounts. "Customized quotes…varies based on unique business needs."
- **Third-party data (G2, Capterra, GetApp, SoftwareAdvice, SoftwareSuggest):** Basic ~$100/mo · Essentials ~$200/mo · Premium ~$300/mo. Transaction fees **3%–4.75%**. Long-term contracts confirmed in review data.
- NFG product page now references an **"Essentials Plan" with a performance guarantee** ("Match or exceed last year's fundraising results — or we'll refund you the difference"). New marketing hook since April.
- **Net:** still gated, still opaque, but a refundable performance guarantee is a new defensive trust play DonorDock should note.

## 8. Attack / Defend / Lateral

### ATTACK (where DonorDock should pressure NFG)
1. **"Bonterra Guided Fundraising" rebrand vocabulary.** Build/expand `/compare/bonterra-guided-fundraising-vs-donordock` and `/alternatives/bonterra-guided-fundraising`. Capture the second-wave rebrand search traffic before competitors do.
2. **Performance-guarantee counter.** NFG's new "match-or-refund" guarantee is a trust signal. Counter with DonorDock's superior asymmetry: no contract, transparent $500/mo flat, 1% fee — published, not gated. A "Real cost of Network for Good vs DonorDock" calculator deserves a refresh with the new $200/mo Essentials + 4.75% fee math.
3. **Pricing transparency wedge stays open.** Bonterra's pricing page still has zero dollar amounts. Every DonorDock page that surfaces "$500/mo flat, 1% fee, no annual contract" gains relative authority.
4. **DonorDock omission from "31+ tools" listicle.** Build an answer-engine-friendly post: "Why DonorDock isn't in Bonterra's roundup (and what they don't tell you)." AEO play; aim for ChatGPT/Perplexity citation.

### DEFEND (where NFG can pressure DonorDock)
1. **Review volume gap.** 937 Capterra + 398 G2 reviews vs. DonorDock's much smaller pool. Drive a Q2 review-generation campaign — target +50 G2 reviews from Action Board users, especially small-shop EDs.
2. **Bonterra Editorial Team byline appearing.** Their E-E-A-T is starting to close. DonorDock must accelerate the planned named-expert byline rollout (Matt Bitzegaio, Rob Burke) before this gap closes further.
3. **NFG's "1:1 fundraising coach" + Bonterra Que AI bundle.** Strong differentiator for buyers who want hand-holding. DonorDock counter: Otto AI + free onboarding + live training (already exists; needs sharper page).
4. **NFG's peer-to-peer + custom fields.** Still feature gaps for DonorDock. If DonorDock can't ship P2P, content should reframe ("most small nonprofits don't run P2P at scale — here's what works instead").

### LATERAL (parallel moves NFG isn't blocking)
1. **Migration-intent cluster.** "Leaving Network for Good," "exporting data from Bonterra," "switching to DonorDock" — Bonterra has zero defensive content here. Open lane.
2. **Short-form AEO answer posts.** Bonterra goes long (15K-word listicles). Short 800–1,200 word direct-answer posts with FAQ schema are still under-served and disproportionately cited by AI engines.
3. **llms.txt + product schema.** DonorDock retains both advantages this cycle. Compound them: enumerate every comparison and pricing URL in llms.txt, ship `SoftwareApplication` + `Offer` + `AggregateRating` on `/pricing` and product pages.
4. **Small-nonprofit-specific topical hub.** NFG's content reads enterprise-y despite SMB claim. Lane stays open for "1–5 staff nonprofit," "ED doing everything," "fundraiser wearing 5 hats" content.

---

## Top 3 actions for May–June 2026
1. **Capture the "Bonterra Guided Fundraising" rebrand search.** Add/expand comparison + alternatives pages using both brand variants. Same page; updated copy + schema.
2. **Ship named-expert author bylines (Matt + Rob) before Bonterra Editorial Team scales.** This window closes if Bonterra adds individual authors next.
3. **Refresh `/compare/network-for-good-vs-donordock`** with current $200/mo Essentials reference, NFG's new performance guarantee, updated G2/Capterra review counts, and `SoftwareApplication` + `AggregateRating` schema.
