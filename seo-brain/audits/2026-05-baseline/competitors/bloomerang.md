- **DonorDock advantage holds:** DD homepage carries SoftwareApplication + Offer + AggregateRating; Bloomerang homepage does not.

## 4. Pricing
**Partially visible — new structure since April.** Now shows starting prices per product:
- Bloomerang Fundraising: starting **$40/mo** (annual)
- Bloomerang CRM: starting **$125/mo** (annual)
- Bloomerang Volunteer: starting **$119/mo** (annual)

This narrows DonorDock's "transparent pricing" attack lane, though full pricing for the bundle is still gated and "constituent-based" still means scaling cost as orgs grow.

## 7. Comparison Page Targeting DonorDock
**No public Bloomerang page targets DonorDock.** Tested and confirmed 404s on:
- `/compare/donordock/` — 404
- `/alternatives/donordock/` — 404
- `/donordock-alternative/` — 404
- `/vs/donordock/` — 404

**MAJOR DELTA from April: Bloomerang DOES now have a competitor-comparison program at `/alternative/{competitor}/`**, but DonorDock is **not yet a target**. Live `/alternative/*` pages now include:
- Blackbaud, Virtuous, eTapestry, Little Green Light, DonorPerfect, Donorbox, VolunteerHub, Bonterra/Salsa, Volunteer Impact, Salesforce for Nonprofits, Network for Good, EveryAction, NeonCRM, Volgistics, SignUpGenius, Raiser's Edge, "Spreadsheets"

Pattern: H1 "THE [COMPETITOR] ALTERNATIVE", "Top reasons nonprofits choose Bloomerang over X," G2 score comparisons (e.g., 9.2 vs 8.2 ease-of-use), feature checklists. No FAQPage schema on these pages yet.

**Implication:** DonorDock has likely been deemed too small to warrant a dedicated comparison page — for now. As DD's search presence grows, this is the next page Bloomerang will build. **DonorDock should claim "Bloomerang alternative" / "vs Bloomerang" SERPs aggressively this quarter while the lane is still wide open.**

## 8. Attack / Defend / Lateral

**Attack (DonorDock pushes here):**
- **"Bloomerang vs DonorDock" SERP** — still uncontested by Bloomerang. Ship the SEO/AEO push now.
- **Migration content** — "Switching from Bloomerang," "Exporting data from Bloomerang." Bloomerang won't write the counter.
- **Constituent-pricing transparency** — "Bloomerang charges per constituent — what does that actually cost at 1K / 5K / 10K records?" Build a calculator. Bloomerang's published $125 CRM start price still scales; flat $500/mo wins above ~1,500 records.
- **Penny vs Otto** — head-to-head AI assistant comparison while Bloomerang is launch-announcement-heavy.
- **FAQPage schema arbitrage** — Bloomerang's flagship still has only 4 Qs; their `/alternative/*` pages have ZERO FAQPage schema. DD can win AEO citations with deeper schema on equivalent pages.
- **Small/growing-nonprofit niche** — Bloomerang content speaks to mid-market. DD owns "first real CRM," "graduating from spreadsheets," "under 1,000 donors."

**Defend (where Bloomerang is genuinely stronger):**
- Topical breadth and decade of domain authority — don't try to out-publish; out-answer.
- Volunteer management content depth (dedicated product → dedicated content moat).
- Review volume (5–6x DD's count). Keep pumping G2/Capterra reviews and surface them in schema.
- "Ask an Expert" bylined-author E-E-A-T. DD needs to elevate Focused Fundraiser podcast hosts as named experts with Person schema and bylines.
- Integration breadth via dedicated `/integrations/` hub.

**Lateral (areas Bloomerang ignores, easy to claim):**
- Faith-based, rural, arts/culture, advocacy verticals.
- "Nonprofit CRM under $X" budget-tier roundups Bloomerang can't credibly write.
- Platform-fee transparency (1% vs typical 3–5% giving-platform fees).
- DIY / spreadsheet migration content for orgs upgrading for the first time.
- Founder-led / Fargo / heartland trust signals (vs PE-owned).
- Podcast/transcript repurposing with VideoObject + Article schema.
