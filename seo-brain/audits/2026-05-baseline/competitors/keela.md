# Keela vs. DonorDock — Competitor SEO Audit (May 2026)

**Date:** 2026-05-04
**Audit Type:** Monthly baseline refresh
**Prior baseline:** 2026-04-22
**Prepared for:** seo-brain strategist system

---

## TL;DR — Biggest Delta vs. April

**Keela is no longer a standalone brand.** It is now the donor-management module of **Velora**, a suite (Aug 2025 launch) combining Aplos (accounting), Raisely (fundraising), and Keela (CRM) — all under Alpine Investors. The keela.co domain is still live and functions as the marketing site for the CRM module, but every footer, careers link, and "about" reference now flows to joinvelora.com. **Strategic implication:** Keela's positioning is shifting from "nonprofit CRM" to "the donor-management piece of a fund-accounting suite." This is a meaningful opening for DonorDock's "one platform, one bill, one login" message — Velora explicitly is not one platform, it's three integrated products.

## 1. Tier

**Mid-market / SMB nonprofit** — overlaps DonorDock's ICP heavily (sub-$5M revenue, 1-10 staff). Now positioned to scale upward via the Velora suite (paired with Aplos accounting + Raisely advanced fundraising). The combined Velora claims **15,000+ nonprofits worldwide** across all three products.

- **6 visible blog pillars:** Nonprofit Fundraising, Donor Management, Donor Communications, Nonprofit Resources, Nonprofit Management, Reporting & Analytics.

- **DELTA vs. April:** No measurable expansion. Content engine appears flat-to-slightly-stale post-acquisition — a known pattern for acquired SaaS marketing teams (Aplos likely consolidating editorial under Velora). **Watch for 2026 publishing slowdown** as a leading indicator.

## 3. Schema Coverage Observed

- **Homepage:** No JSON-LD structured data visible in fetched source. Surprising for a Webflow-built marketing site; likely Organization schema is present in the head but was not surfaced.
- **Blog posts:** Article/BlogPosting schema present (consistent with prior baseline observation).
- **/tools and resource pages:** No CreativeWork or HowTo schema observed.
- **Pricing page:** No Product or Offer schema observed despite full pricing being public — a missed AEO opportunity.
- **Comparison/competitor pages:** Not present on keela.co (see #7).
- **DELTA vs. April:** No change. Schema posture remains a weakness DonorDock can exploit with structured Offer, Product, FAQPage, and ComparisonTable schema on its own pricing + comparison pages.

## 8. Attack / Defend / Lateral Notes

### Attack (where DonorDock should press)

1. **"Suite vs. one platform" wedge — NEW in May.** Velora is explicitly three products being stitched together via "expanding integrations." DonorDock's "one login, one bill, one place for your data" is a sharper story than ever. Build a comparison angle: *"Why nonprofits choose one tool over a suite of three."*
2. **Publish `/compare/donordock-vs-keela` and `/keela-alternatives`.** Still uncontested by Keela. Add structured Offer + ComparisonTable + FAQPage schema. Target uplift: position 1-3 within 60 days of publishing.
3. **US compliance content cluster.** Keela's Canadian heritage = under-investment in IRS/501(c)(3)/Form 990/state charitable registration content. Same recommendation as April, still open.
4. **Transparent pricing TCO content.** Keela's tiered model + add-ons ($200/mo Premium Care, gated Pipelines/Texting/Wealth Screening) creates total-cost-of-ownership ambiguity. Build a "true cost of Keela vs. DonorDock at 1K, 2.5K, 5K contacts" calculator/page.
5. **Acquisition-aware migration content.** "Switch from Keela" + "Keela acquired by Aplos — what it means for you" — capture customers nervous about consolidation roadmap risk.

### Defend (where DonorDock is exposed)

1. **Top-of-funnel education volume.** Keela's "hundreds of articles" + Academy archive still outweighs DonorDock's blog. Even if cadence is slowing post-acquisition, the back-catalog ranks. Continue building the FF Article Pipeline cadence.
2. **Free template/lead-magnet library.** Keela's /tools hub is a working lead engine; DonorDock has minimal equivalent. Build at least 5 templates Q2 (donation receipt, thank-you letter, year-end appeal, board report, donor retention calculator).
3. **AI-for-nonprofits narrative.** Keela is leaning in. DonorDock's Otto positioning needs more visible top-of-funnel content (pillar page + at least 4 supporting articles in next 60 days).
4. **Wealth screening + Pipelines.** Keela offers these as add-ons; DonorDock customers asking for prospect research / major gift workflow features may compare unfavorably. Tighten the "growing fundraising teams" objection-handling.

### Lateral (don't waste cycles)

1. **Canadian / CRA / bilingual content.** Keela's geographic moat. Don't compete.
2. **15K+ nonprofit suite scale claims.** Velora will out-trumpet DonorDock on logo counts. Compete on outcomes and fit, not scale.
3. **Native fund accounting (Aplos).** DonorDock integrates with QuickBooks; Velora has Aplos baked in. Don't try to match — emphasize CRM-first focus and partner-friendly accounting integration story.
