## 8. Attack / Defend / Lateral Notes

### ATTACK (where DonorDock should pressure Virtuous this cycle)
- **Pricing transparency narrative.** Virtuous's pricing is still gated. Publish a "Real cost of Virtuous vs. DonorDock for a $750K nonprofit" article + calculator. Use the SelectHub-confirmed $60/user/mo and $100/user/mo data points.
- **Build `/compare/virtuous-vs-donordock` NOW.** Still unbuilt. Affiliates (Zeffy, SelectHub, charityengine.net) are filling the gap and Virtuous itself isn't targeting it. First-mover SERP is wide open.
- **Counter-program their Benchmark Report.** Virtuous owns "median gift up 20%" headline this month. DonorDock's lean-team data set (smaller orgs Virtuous doesn't have access to) can publish a counter-statistic — e.g., "What lean-team nonprofits actually raised in 2026" — within 30 days while the news cycle is hot.
- **AI for small teams.** Momentum's new "AI agents triple gift officer outreach" assumes a major-gift officer exists. Otto's pitch for orgs without a dedicated MGO is a defensible differentiator — push content that says so explicitly.

### DEFEND (where DonorDock is exposed)
- **Schema + AEO parity.** Virtuous still ships FAQPage + Article schema on pillars; DonorDock is thin. Top 30 articles should be retrofitted this quarter — closable in one sprint.
- **Owned-worldview gap.** "Responsive Fundraising" is unchallenged. "Focused Fundraising" (podcast brand) and "Smart Steward Method" (page exists) need to be unified into one named, gated, defensible framework before Virtuous expands category claim.
- **Annual flagship asset.** Virtuous just dropped Benchmark Report + got Morningstar/PR Newswire pickup. DonorDock has no annual flagship. The DonorDock Benchmark for Lean Nonprofits remains the single highest-leverage missing asset — recommend kickoff this quarter.
- **Vertical pages (faith-based mid-sized, K-12 small private school, small human services).** DonorDock has zero. Virtuous has 5. Even one well-built vertical page would close part of the gap.

### LATERAL (don't fight here)
- **Enterprise/$5M+ segment.** Virtuous owns it; not DonorDock's fight.
- **RNS / annual user summit at scale.** Don't try to match. DonorDock's "Focused Fundraiser" virtual event (smaller, lean-team-specific) is the right asymmetric play.
- **Major-gift / wealth-screening content.** Virtuous, iWave, DonorSearch, GivingDNA own this. DonorDock should reference, not compete on, MGO content.
- **Healthcare / higher ed enterprise verticals.** Virtuous has dedicated benchmarks; DonorDock can ignore until clear ICP demand emerges.
