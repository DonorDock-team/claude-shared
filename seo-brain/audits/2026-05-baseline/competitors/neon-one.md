## 4. Pricing Observed
**Visible and gated mix.** A public pricing page exists at neonone.com/solutions/neon-crm-overview/neon-crm-pricing/, but exact tier pricing is most reliably reproduced on G2, Capterra, ITQlick, and SoftwareFinder. Three-tier revenue-based model:

| Tier | Nonprofit | Association | What's added |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essentials | **$99/mo** | $109/mo | 360 constituent mgmt, donation pages, 3 workflows, basic reporting, PCI |
| Impact | **$209/mo** | $219/mo | + Volunteers, Events, 15 workflows, open API, online store |
| Empower | **$409/mo** | $439/mo | + Radius search, live chat & phone support, unlimited workflows, NCOA, expert hour |

Plus: revenue-based price step-ups, ~3% processing fee, $25/mo donation acceptance add-on, +10% Memberships/Volunteers, +20% Events.

**DELTA vs April:** Association tier prices ($109 / $219 / $439) are now publicly indexed and showing alongside nonprofit tiers. This is a small but real expansion of pricing transparency — the spread between nonprofit and association pricing (~$10–$30/mo) is a new wedge for any "association-friendly DonorDock" angle if we ever pursue that segment (we don't — note only).
