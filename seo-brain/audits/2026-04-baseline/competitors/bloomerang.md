## 1. Executive Summary

- **Bloomerang is the content incumbent.** ~1,300+ blog posts, 152 marketing pages, 72 long-form guides, 200+ customer stories, dedicated sitemaps for webinars/infographics/templates/news/topics. DonorDock's sitemap: ~619 URLs. Bloomerang has roughly **3-4x the indexable content surface** and a decade of domain authority.
- **Bloomerang's biggest SEO strength is topical breadth on evergreen nonprofit fundraising terms** — "nonprofit CRM," "donor database," "moves management," "nonprofit software," "fundraising apps," "peer-to-peer fundraising," "capital campaign," "volunteer management," "major gifts." They own listicle and "best of" real estate with 4-year-old URLs ranking via internal linking authority.
- **Bloomerang's biggest AEO/GEO weakness is pricing opacity and reactive AI posture.** Their /pricing/ page is paywalled behind a 403 to scrapers with no transparent tier labels in HTML, and their robots.txt only whitelists `SiteAuditBot` — they do not actively welcome GPTBot/ClaudeBot/Perplexity. Their llms.txt is thin (78 lines, surface-level, auto-generated by Yoast).
- **DonorDock's biggest live-site gap (per Rob's direct verification — agent overstated this) is a robots.txt vs llms.txt conflict.** DonorDock robots.txt explicitly blocks ClaudeBot/GPTBot/etc. while llms.txt invites them ("Training: allowed"). Bots respect robots.txt first — reconcile immediately.
- **DonorDock's winning response is asymmetric warfare: don't try to out-publish, out-answer.** Own the "vs Bloomerang," "Bloomerang alternative," "Bloomerang pricing calculator," "Bloomerang for small nonprofits," and "exporting data from Bloomerang" queries. Add FAQPage schema to all articles. Publish transparent-pricing comparison pages. Target AEO/GEO first (where DonorDock's llms.txt and schema already lead), then scale SEO content around small-nonprofit niche Bloomerang under-serves.

## 4. Technical SEO Posture

### robots.txt

**Bloomerang:**
```
User-agent: *
Disallow: /wp-admin/
User-agent: SiteAuditBot
Allow: /
Sitemap: https://bloomerang.co/sitemap.xml  ← POINTS TO LEGACY .CO DOMAIN
```
- Does NOT explicitly allow or disallow AI crawlers. Neutral/implicit allow under `User-agent: *`
- No Cloudflare Content-Signal directives
- **Bug:** Sitemap URL still points to legacy bloomerang.co

**DonorDock:**
- Uses Cloudflare Content-Signal protocol: `search=yes,ai-train=no`
- Explicitly **disallows** ClaudeBot, GPTBot, Google-Extended, Amazonbot, Applebot-Extended, Bytespider, CCBot, meta-externalagent
- **Conflict with DonorDock's own llms.txt** (states "Training: allowed, Commercial use: allowed"). Bots respect robots.txt first. **Must be reconciled** — allow GPTBot/ClaudeBot/PerplexityBot for live grounding while optionally keeping ai-train=no.

### Sitemap structure
- **Bloomerang:** Proper index with 13 child sitemaps segmented by content type. Yoast-generated. Best practice.
- **DonorDock:** Flat single urlset with 619 URLs. No type segmentation. No `<lastmod>` tags. No image/video sitemap extensions. **Weaker signal architecture.**

### Canonical / meta hygiene (homepage)

| Signal | Bloomerang home | DonorDock home |
|---|---|---|
| `<title>` | ✓ | ✓ |
| meta description | ✓ | ✓ (agent said missing — Rob's direct check found 134-char description. Verify.) |
| canonical | ✓ | agent said missing — verify |
| OpenGraph (og:title, og:description, og:image, og:url) | ✓ | partial per agent — verify |
| Twitter card | ✓ | agent said missing — verify |

### Schema markup (JSON-LD)

**Bloomerang homepage:** 1 JSON-LD block with: Organization, WebSite, WebPage, BreadcrumbList, ListItem, ImageObject, EntryPoint (SearchAction + ReadAction), PostalAddress, PropertyValueSpecification, QuantitativeValue.
- **Missing on homepage:** SoftwareApplication, Product, Offer, AggregateRating, Review.
- **Bloomerang blog posts** add: Article, Person, FAQPage, Question, Answer, VideoObject, CommentAction.

**DonorDock homepage:** 2 JSON-LD blocks: Organization, SoftwareApplication, Offer, AggregateRating, WebPage, ContactPoint, ImageObject.
- **DonorDock wins** on homepage product schema (eligible for rich results Bloomerang can't get).
- **DonorDock articles** use BlogPosting + Person + Organization but **no FAQPage, no HowTo, no VideoObject, no BreadcrumbList**.

**Gaps both sites share:**
- Neither homepage has FAQPage schema
- Neither has Review schema beyond AggregateRating
- DonorDock articles missing FAQPage — high-value AEO win (Bloomerang's flagship article has only 4 Qs, parity + depth is easily achievable)

Lists top pages, 5 recent posts, 5 customer stories, 5 guides, 5 infographics, 5 landing pages, 5 news items, 5 templates, 5 webinars, 5 topics. **No pricing. No company description. **DonorDock (118 lines):** Hand-written, strategic. - DonorDock flagship "Best Nonprofit CRM 2026" = **0 FAQ schema.** Gap to close.

- **Bloomerang:** Listicles — LLMs love these for grounding. Decade of topical authority.
- **DonorDock:** Original frameworks (Relationship Loop, Smart Steward Method, Moves Management for Small Teams), explicit pricing, verifiable reviews (4.8/5, 200+), named founders, SOC 2 — LLMs favor structured citable claims with specific numbers.

## 9. Strategic Recommendations for DonorDock

### Priority 1 — Reconcile robots.txt vs llms.txt conflict (this week)
Allow `GPTBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `PerplexityBot`, `Google-Extended` in robots.txt to match the llms.txt invitation. You want to be cited.

### Priority 2 — Ship FAQPage schema across the site (1-2 weeks)
- /faq (114+ Qs) with FAQPage schema — dozens of Google "People Also Ask" placements possible
- /articles/best-nonprofit-crm (beat Bloomerang's 4 Qs with 10-15 deep specific Qs)
- Every /compare/* page

### Priority 3 — Triple down on comparison and migration content (4-8 weeks)
Bloomerang publishes zero head-to-head comparisons. Own the category. Add: "Switching from Bloomerang to DonorDock," "Bloomerang pricing explained" (objective explainer — AEO loves these), "How to export your data from Bloomerang," "Bloomerang alternative for small nonprofits."

### Priority 4 — Convert features into content pillars (2-3 months)
Top 10 features (moves management, recurring donations, online giving, text outreach, grants, volunteer management, major gifts, stewardship journeys, Otto AI, ActionBoard) each get 1,500-2,500-word pillar articles with FAQPage schema, named author with Person schema, cross-links.

### Priority 5 — Attack Bloomerang's pricing opacity as a content weapon
- Publish "The true cost of nonprofit CRM" (transparency piece)
- Build interactive "Nonprofit CRM pricing calculator" showing Bloomerang's effective cost at 500/1,000/5,000/10,000 contacts vs DonorDock's flat rate

### Priority 6 — Lean into AEO/GEO while Bloomerang is slow
- Expand llms.txt to include direct answers to common buying questions
- Publish "answer-ready" blocks on every pillar page (TL;DR + explicit Q&A sections)
- Add VideoObject schema to Focused Fundraiser podcast episodes with transcripts

### Priority 7 — Claim the small-nonprofit niche Bloomerang cedes
Ship "Small Nonprofit CRM" content cluster: "Best CRM for nonprofits under 500 donors," "CRM for one-person fundraising shops," "CRM for faith-based nonprofits," "CRM for rural community foundations."
