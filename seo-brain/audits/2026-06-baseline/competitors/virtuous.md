## 8. Attack / Defend / Lateral Notes

### ATTACK (where DonorDock should pressure Virtuous this cycle)
- **Build `/compare/virtuous-vs-donordock` NOW.** Open since at least April; affiliates own the SERP and Virtuous still isn't targeting it. Highest-leverage open attack — the post-summit lull is the moment to ship while Virtuous's content team is heads-down on recaps.
- **Pricing transparency narrative.** Still gated. Publish "Real cost of Virtuous vs. DonorDock for a $750K nonprofit" + calculator using SelectHub $60/$100 per-user data.
- **AI for small teams.** Momentum's pitch ("manage 5x more relationships") assumes a dedicated major-gift officer. The AI Adoption Report's own stat — only 7% of orgs see major AI impact — is ammunition: DonorDock's Otto for orgs *without* an MGO is the asymmetric counter-narrative.
- **Counter-program the Benchmark Report** while it's still the live headline ("median gift up 20%"). DonorDock's lean-team data ("what lean-team nonprofits actually raised in 2026") still unshipped — window narrowing.

### DEFEND (where DonorDock is exposed)
- **Schema + AEO parity.** Virtuous still ships FAQPage + Article on pillars; DonorDock thin. Retrofit top 30 articles this quarter — one sprint.
- **Owned-worldview gap.** "Responsive Fundraising" unchallenged and Respond '26 just amplified it to the *entire* fundraising community (not just customers). Unify "Focused Fundraising" + "Smart Steward Method" into one named, gated framework before Virtuous's category claim widens further.
- **Annual flagship asset.** Virtuous runs TWO research franchises (Fundraising Benchmark + AI Adoption). DonorDock has none. The DonorDock Benchmark for Lean Nonprofits remains the single highest-leverage missing asset.
- **Vertical pages.** DonorDock has zero; Virtuous has 5. One well-built vertical page closes part of the gap.

### LATERAL (don't fight here)
- **Enterprise/$5M+ segment.** Virtuous owns it.
- **Respond '26 / large user summit.** Don't match scale; DonorDock's "Focused Fundraiser" virtual event is the right asymmetric play.
- **Major-gift / wealth-screening / AI-agent-for-MGO content.** Virtuous, Momentum, iWave, DonorSearch own this. Reference, don't compete.
- **Healthcare / higher-ed enterprise verticals.** Ignore until clear ICP demand.
