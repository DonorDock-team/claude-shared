## 8. Attack / Defend / Lateral

### ATTACK
1. **"Bonterra Guided Fundraising" rebrand search.** G2 now canonicalizes this name. Build/expand `/compare/bonterra-guided-fundraising-vs-donordock` and `/alternatives/bonterra-guided-fundraising` with both brand variants — capture rebrand-driven queries.
2. **Pricing transparency wedge stays wide open.** Bonterra still shows $0 amounts; reviews now cite cost as a top complaint. Every DonorDock page surfacing "$500/mo flat, 1% fee, no contract — published" gains relative authority. Refresh the "real cost" calculator with $200/mo Essentials + 4.75% fee math.
3. **DonorDock omission from "34-tool" listicle.** Ship AEO post: "Why DonorDock isn't in Bonterra's roundup (and what they don't tell you)." Aim for ChatGPT/Perplexity citation.
4. **Review-velocity dip.** Bonterra's Capterra count is flat MoM. A focused DonorDock Q2/Q3 review push narrows the gap faster than usual right now.

### DEFEND
1. **Review-volume moat (~10x on Capterra).** Still the biggest threat. Drive +50 G2 reviews from Action Board users, especially small-shop EDs.
2. **Bonterra Que AI + 1:1 coaching bundle.** Strong hand-holding differentiator. Counter: Otto AI + free onboarding + live training; sharpen the page.
3. **Peer-to-peer + custom fields.** Still feature gaps. If P2P won't ship, reframe ("most small nonprofits don't run P2P at scale — here's what works").

### LATERAL
1. **Migration-intent cluster.** "Leaving Network for Good," "exporting data from Bonterra," "switching to DonorDock." Bonterra has zero defensive content. Open lane.
2. **Short-form AEO answer posts (800–1,200 words + FAQ schema).** Bonterra goes long; short direct-answer posts are under-served and over-cited by AI engines.
3. **llms.txt + product schema.** DonorDock retains both edges (Bonterra llms.txt still 404; no SoftwareApplication/Offer/AggregateRating). Compound them.
4. **Small-nonprofit topical hub.** NFG content reads enterprise-y despite SMB claim. "1–5 staff," "ED doing everything" lane stays open.
