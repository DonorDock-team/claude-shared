# Keela vs. DonorDock — Competitor SEO Audit (June 2026)

**Competitor:** Keela (keela.co) — now "Velora CRM" within the Velora suite
**Date:** 2026-06-01
**Audit Type:** Monthly baseline refresh
**Prior baseline:** 2026-05-04
**Prepared for:** seo-brain strategist system

---

## TL;DR — Biggest Delta vs. May

**The Velora absorption is accelerating from "footer mention" to active co-branding.** In May, keela.co was a standalone marketing site with a footer nod to Velora. As of June, joinvelora.com now hosts a dedicated **`/keela` page headlined "Keela is Velora CRM,"** and the suite homepage rebrands all three products as **Velora CRM (Keela), Velora Fundraising (Raisely), and Velora Fund Accounting (Aplos)**. keela.co still resolves independently and has NOT been redirected/sunset — but the trajectory is clear: Keela the brand is being subordinated to "Velora CRM." **Strategic implication:** the "one platform vs. three products stitched together" wedge is now even sharper — Velora's own homepage says "Each product is powerful on its own. Together..." (i.e., three products), explicitly confirming DonorDock's "one platform, one login, one bill" contrast. This window is still open: no Keela/Velora comparison page targets DonorDock, and DonorDock still has NO `/compare/donordock-vs-keela` page.

## 1. Tier / Velora Status

**Mid-market / SMB nonprofit** — still overlaps DonorDock's ICP (sub-$5M revenue, 1-10 staff). Keela is the **donor-management / CRM layer of Velora**, now formally co-branded "Velora CRM." Velora still claims **15,000+ nonprofits** across all three products (North America, Australia, beyond). Keela retains its own product team, support, and pricing (no customer disruption messaged), but brand identity is migrating under the Velora umbrella.

- **DELTA vs. May:** Positioning hardened from "secondary footer relationship" → active dual-branding ("Keela by Velora" / "Keela is Velora CRM") on joinvelora.com. The standalone brand erosion is now visible and accelerating.

## 8. Attack / Defend / Lateral Notes

### Attack
1. **"Suite vs. one platform" wedge — now CONFIRMED by Velora's own copy.** joinvelora.com literally says "Each product is powerful on its own. Together..." — three products. DonorDock's "one login, one bill, one place" is sharper than ever. Build: *"Why nonprofits choose one tool over a suite of three."*
2. **Ship `/compare/donordock-vs-keela` AND `/keela-alternatives` + a `/velora-alternatives` variant.** Still uncontested. Add Offer + ComparisonTable + FAQPage schema. Target position 1-3 within 60 days.
3. **Acquisition/rebrand-anxiety migration content.** "Keela is becoming Velora CRM — what it means for your subscription" — capture customers nervous about consolidation/roadmap risk. Higher intent now that co-branding is live.
4. **US compliance cluster** (IRS / 501(c)(3) / Form 990 / state charitable registration) — Keela's Canadian heritage = under-investment. Still open.
5. **TCO transparency** — Keela's tiers + gated add-ons ($200/mo Premium Care, Pipelines/Texting/Wealth Screening) create cost ambiguity, made worse by Velora's contradictory "no complicated add-ons" claim. Build a "true cost of Keela/Velora at 1K/2.5K/5K contacts" page.

### Defend
1. **Top-of-funnel volume.** Keela's back-catalog still outranks DonorDock even with flat cadence. Continue FF Article Pipeline.
2. **Free template/lead-magnet library.** Keela's /tools hub still a working lead engine; build 5+ DonorDock templates Q2.
3. **AI-for-nonprofits narrative.** Keela still leaning in. Push Otto top-of-funnel content (pillar + 4 supporting articles).
4. **Wealth screening + Pipelines + Raisely campaign tooling.** Velora can now point to deeper fundraising/major-gift tooling across the suite. Tighten "growing fundraising teams" objection handling.

### Lateral (don't chase)
1. Canadian / CRA / bilingual content — geographic moat.
2. 15K+ nonprofit suite scale claims — compete on outcomes/fit, not logo counts.
3. Native fund accounting (Aplos/Velora Fund Accounting) — emphasize CRM-first focus + partner-friendly QuickBooks integration; don't try to match.
