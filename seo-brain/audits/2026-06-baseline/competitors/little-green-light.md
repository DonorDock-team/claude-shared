## 1. Tier
**SMB nonprofit.** Unchanged. US-only (geo-blocks EU/UK/CH). Small-to-mid nonprofits under ~$2M budget, 1–5 staff. ICP overlap with DonorDock remains ~90%.

## 2. Content Engine
- **Page sitemap:** **184 entries** (May = 182 → **+2 pages, +1%**). The +34% surge from April→May has **stopped cold**. May expansion looks like a one-time push, not a sustained program.
- **Post sitemap:** ~150–500 blog posts (individual post URLs still carry a stale 2022-03-28 Yoast lastmod — the long-standing sitemap quirk persists; content is fresher than dates imply).
- **Cadence:** Healthy and steady ~1 post/week. Latest: "LGL launches new online user community" (May 27), "Lapsed donor data" (May 21), "Enhanced Themes for forms" (May 13), "Neuroscience for fundraising" (May 7), "LGL Email Builder" (Apr 30).
- **New signal:** May 27 post announces a **new online user community** — a retention/engagement play, not an SEO surface, but worth watching as a moat-builder.
- **Topical pillars (unchanged):** practitioner fundraising how-tos. Editorial tone, not commercial. Still zero AI/automation vocabulary.
- **DELTA:** Page growth flattened (+1%). They invested in May and paused. Blog cadence flat. No new commercial/AEO content.

## 3. Schema Coverage
- **Sitemap/SEO engine:** Yoast SEO, now **v27.6** (was v27.4 in May) — routine plugin bump, no schema feature change.
- **Homepage JSON-LD (unchanged):** WebPage, WebSite (SearchAction), Organization (sameAs), ImageObject, BreadcrumbList. Article schema on posts (Yoast default).
- **Still missing:** SoftwareApplication, Product, AggregateRating, FAQPage, foundingDate.
- **DELTA:** **No schema improvements.** The Yoast version bump did not add Product/SoftwareApplication/FAQ/AggregateRating. The AEO/GEO opening DonorDock identified in April remains **wide open** — now 3 months running.

## 6. Pillar Pages LGL Owns
Unchanged set: **/pricing/**, six+ vertical pages (animal-care, human-services, arts, land-trusts, schools, libraries, + juniorleague/landscape niches), /online-donations/, /why-lgl/, /reviews/, /migrate-to-lgl/, /consultants-network/, ~20+ webinar landings, ~25+ ebook/guide gates, blog category hubs, plus category pages /donor-management-software/ and /nonprofit-donor-management-software/.
- **DELTA:** Pillar set effectively unchanged. The 46 pages added in May did not create new strategic pillars — likely additional webinar/guide assets reinforcing existing clusters.

## 7. Comparison Page Targeting DonorDock?
- **No.** Page-sitemap confirms still **only one** competitor alternative URL: **/kindful-alternative/**. No donordock-, bloomerang-, donorperfect-, neon-, salesforce-, bonterra-, givebutter- alternative pages.
- DonorDock continues to publish 10 comparison pages including a live **/compare/little-green-light-vs-donordock**.
- Third-party surfaces (Capterra, G2, SelectHub, TrustRadius) actively rank "DonorDock vs LGL" comparisons.
- **DELTA:** **None.** LGL has built zero defensive comparison pages in 60+ days. DonorDock's 10-to-1 comparison-SEO advantage holds.

## 8. Attack / Defend / Lateral

**ATTACK (where DD presses):**
- **Schema gap now 3 months stale.** LGL still has no SoftwareApplication/Product/AggregateRating/FAQPage. Ship these on DD homepage, /pricing, /compare/*, /crm pillar now to own rich-result + AI-citation real estate while LGL coasts.
- **AI-search / GEO blind spot persists.** robots.txt still has no AI-bot allow/disallow directives; llms.txt is thin Yoast-auto (v27.6). No GEO investment.
- **Page-growth stalled.** The May surge was one-and-done — momentum is not compounding. DD's own content velocity now compares more favorably.
- **Modern/AI/easy keyword cluster.** LGL content is still 100% practitioner how-to, zero AI vocabulary. Wide open.

**DEFEND (where LGL presses DD):**
- **Capterra 316 reviews + 4.9 value score.** Still their loudest signal in "best donor CRM under $X" AI answers. Note: count is flat — keep DD review velocity high to close the gap. Keep TCO framing on every comparison surface so $45 vs $98+ never appears without context.
- **New user community + 15-yr tenure + consultant ecosystem.** The May 27 community launch is a retention moat. Their affiliate/consultant backlinks keep compounding on head terms — flank, don't out-link.

**LATERAL (where DD goes around them):**
- Verticals LGL lacks: churches/faith-based, healthcare nonprofits, advocacy, environmental, food banks.
- Size-based pages: "donor CRM under $500k budget," "for solo development director."
- "Leaving Little Green Light" migration hub (recommended since April — still unshipped, still open).
- Mobile / text-to-give / SMS SEO surface — LGL barely mentions mobile.
- Mid-funnel doubt queries: "is LGL worth it?", "problems with LGL."
