## Priority tier definitions

- **P0** — Direct commercial / transactional intent. High buyer proximity. Own within 90 days. Target 1-page-1 SERP.
- **P1** — High-intent informational + category education. Own within 6 months. Target 1-page-1 for long-tail, 1-page-2 for heads.
- **P2** — Long-tail, brand-building, AEO-adjacent. Own within 12 months. Comprehensive coverage over individual rankings.

## Prohibited keywords (per brand-positioning.md)

Do NOT target:
- "Small nonprofit CRM" / "first CRM" / "solo ED CRM"
- "Starting a nonprofit"
- "Church CRM," "tithing software," "congregational giving"
- "Cheapest nonprofit CRM" (reframe as "transparent pricing" angle instead)

Faith-based (non-church) keywords ARE OK: "faith-based nonprofit CRM," "parachurch nonprofit software," "religious social services fundraising," etc.

## Prohibited content angles

- Pure price competition ("cheapest," "under $100") — we're upmarket
- Volunteer-run org content (below ICP)
- Churches as target audience (Planning Center owns)
- First-time nonprofit founders (not ICP)
