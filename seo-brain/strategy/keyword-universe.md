## Structure (per keyword)

- Term
- Cluster
- Pillar (maps to `pillars.md`)
- Search intent (informational / commercial / transactional / navigational)
- Monthly search volume
- Difficulty score
- Current ranking position (if any)
- Target URL (existing or to-build)
- Priority tier (P0, P1, P2)

## Priority tiers

- **P0** — Direct commercial/transactional keywords with buying intent ("donor management software," "nonprofit CRM")
- **P1** — High-intent informational queries we can answer authoritatively
- **P2** — Long-tail AEO / brand-building queries
