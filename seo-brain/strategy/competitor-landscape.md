# Competitor Landscape

**Locked:** 2026-04-23 (Phase 2 launch)
**Source:** 8 competitor audits in `seo-brain/audits/2026-04-baseline/competitors/` + `brand-positioning.md` rules
**Owner:** Rob Burke (CMO)
**Refresh cadence:** Quarterly (next: 2026-07-22)

This document is the canonical competitive intelligence map. Every new compare page, battlecard, ad, and article should cross-reference this doc before making claims about a named competitor.

---

## Competitor tier map

### Tier 1 — Direct head-to-head, ICP overlap, active threat
- **Virtuous** — biggest content-engine threat, upmarket into DonorDock's upper ICP
- **Givebutter** — only competitor actively attacking DonorDock's brand SERP
- **Bloomerang** — category incumbent, owns evergreen SEO real estate

### Tier 2 — Direct, meaningful ICP overlap
- **Bonterra / Network for Good** — mentions DonorDock dismissively in their content
- **DonorPerfect** — large modular platform, weak on author E-E-A-T
- **Neon One** — tiered suite with add-on % markups

### Tier 3 — Adjacent or receding
- **Little Green Light** — tenured but exiting AI-search surface
- **Keela** — Canadian-origin, education-heavy; no /vs/donordock page

## Per-competitor snapshot

### Virtuous (virtuous.org) — Tier 1 🔴
**Position:** "Responsive Fundraising" category owner. Book + playbook + annual RNS summit + 5 vertical benchmark reports + Chief AI Officer.
**Content footprint:** 646 blog posts + 187 pages + 75 case studies + 41 gated resources (2.8–10x DonorDock by asset type).
**ICP overlap:** Virtuous Platform tier targets up to $5M in fundraising revenue — direct overlap with DonorDock's upper ICP.
**Pricing:** Enterprise tier starting ~$8,000/year (~$667/mo) as of 2026 per Rob; historically was ~$199/mo entry. Upmarket shift aligns them more directly with DonorDock's mid-market segment rather than the small-nonprofit entry tier.
**Technical posture:** WordPress + AIOSEO Pro + Elementor. Rich JSON-LD (FAQPage, Article, BreadcrumbList) on articles. Permissive AI bot posture.
**DonorDock vs page:** NEITHER party publishes one. First-mover wins the SERP.
**Threat:** Owns the category language. Will continue pulling mid-market fundraising vocabulary into "Responsive Fundraising" orbit. If DonorDock doesn't establish Smart Stewardship as a counter-category within 6 months, Virtuous defines the conversation.
**Attack surface:** Enterprise-flavored content doesn't speak to growing-nonprofit operational reality. No compare page against DonorDock.

### Givebutter (givebutter.com) — Tier 1 🔴
**Position:** Freemium with 3% platform fee (donor-tip-offset model). Consumer-friendly UI.
**Content footprint:** Heavy commercial + viral-social content. 871 reviews vs DonorDock's 33 on GetApp.
**ICP overlap:** Low-budget, event-driven, consumer-facing nonprofits (below DonorDock's mid-market direction — but Givebutter is moving upmarket).
**Pricing:** Freemium platform + 3% platform fee with optional tips ("free" if donors tip to cover).
**Technical posture:** Permissive to AI crawlers. Strong comparison-page program.
**DonorDock vs page:** **Ranks #1 for "givebutter vs donordock"** at [givebutter.com/alternatives/donordock](https://givebutter.com/alternatives/donordock). Uses a shared template (not custom attack) — **defensible**.
**Threat:** Social-proof gap is real (871 vs 33 reviews). Tipping model attracts nonprofit buyers who later churn when their donors object to the surprise 3% charge — but this takes months to surface.
**Attack surface:** Public reviews document donor-trust issues, "dark pattern" complaints, surprise charges. Tipping model is their documented vulnerability. DonorDock's flat $500 + 1% wins TCO at 2,500+ contacts — but that math isn't being told.

### Bloomerang (bloomerang.com) — Tier 1 🟡
**Position:** "Retention-first CRM" — original brand promise. Mid-market platform play with Volunteer Mgmt, Giving, Website, Coaching products.
**Content footprint:** ~2,000 indexable URLs (1,306 blog posts + 152 marketing pages + 72 long-form guides + 100+ case studies + segmented sitemaps for webinars, infographics, templates, news, topics).
**ICP overlap:** Contact-tier pricing penalizes growth; DonorDock's flat rate becomes rational at 2,500+ contacts.
**Pricing:** Opaque. /pricing returns 403 to scrapers. Tiered by contact count, entry ~$99/mo scaling to $500+/mo quickly, enterprise custom.
**Technical posture:** WordPress + Yoast SEO v27.4. Auto-generated llms.txt (78 lines, surface-level). Robots.txt implicitly allows AI bots. **No SoftwareApplication/AggregateRating/FAQPage schema on homepage** — DonorDock's homepage schema is actually richer.
**DonorDock vs page:** Bloomerang publishes ZERO public comparison pages. DonorDock ranks #1 for "Bloomerang vs DonorDock."
**Threat:** Content-volume incumbent. Decade of domain authority on "nonprofit CRM," "donor database," "moves management," "volunteer scheduling," "fundraising apps." But it's a retention-message competitor, not a worldview competitor.
**Attack surface:** Pricing opacity is content ammunition. "Bloomerang pricing calculator," "How much does Bloomerang cost at 2,500 contacts," "Bloomerang alternative for growing nonprofits." Weak E-E-A-T schema vs DonorDock.

### Bonterra / Network for Good (bonterratech.com/product/network-for-good) — Tier 2 🟡
**Position:** Now consolidated as "Bonterra Network for Good" product line. 10-product suite. Legacy NFG domain 301-redirects to Bonterra.
**Content footprint:** ~1,600–1,800 URLs across Bonterra product lines (993 blog posts + 252 case studies + 185 training). Dilute small-nonprofit focus.
**Direct attack:** **[Mentions DonorDock by name dismissively](https://www.bonterratech.com/blog/nonprofit-crm-guide)** — describes DonorDock as "entry-level" with "limited communication features" in /blog/nonprofit-crm-guide. DonorDock is also **omitted** from Bonterra's newer "31+ top solutions for 2026" listicle (written in 2025-2026).
**Pricing:** Not publicly transparent. Historical NFG 3% platform fee; Bonterra pricing gated.
**Technical posture:** Permissive robots.txt (no explicit AI bot rules). FAQPage schema on NFG product page but MISSING SoftwareApplication/AggregateRating/Offer. Blog posts have Person schema but **no visible author bylines** — major E-E-A-T gap.
**DonorDock's existing compare page:** `/compare/network-for-good-vs-donordock` — recently updated to fix stale $79/mo pricing reference (some hidden-table instances may still need cleanup).
**Threat:** Direct brand attack in their content. Suite-based positioning. Bloated acquisitions.
**Attack surface:** Author E-E-A-T weakness (no bylines on 993 posts). Suite-complexity attack. Migration intent content ("leaving Bonterra").

### DonorPerfect (donorperfect.com) — Tier 2 🟡
**Position:** "We make it easy to do good." 20-year incumbent. 75,000 professionals / 11,000 orgs / 25+ sectors.
**Content footprint:** ~800 URLs (648 blog posts + 157 static pages). 265 posts tagged "fundraising-software."
**ICP overlap:** Mid-market-leaning (0–500 up to 200,001+ constituents). Competes with DonorDock in 50–1,000 donor band.
**Pricing:** Opaque. Three tiers (Core / Plus / Pro). Starts ~$450/mo. Modular add-ons for auctions, crowdfunding, moves mgmt, QuickBooks tier-gated.
**Technical posture:** Yoast default (clean but minimal). NO SoftwareApplication/AggregateRating/FAQPage/Product schema. Robots.txt fully open, no llms.txt. **Zero author bylines on 648 blog posts** — massive E-E-A-T gap.
**DonorDock vs page:** DP publishes comparison pages for Keela/Bloomerang/Raiser's Edge/Salesforce/Kindful — but NOT DonorDock. Uncontested SERP.
**Threat:** Content volume + domain age. Strong on "grant writing," "capital campaigns," "Giving Tuesday" seasonal content.
**Attack surface:** No author bylines = easy E-E-A-T leapfrog. Modular pricing = "DonorPerfect hidden costs" content opportunity. No schema markup = rich-result leapfrog.

### Neon One (neonone.com) — Tier 2 🟡
**Position:** "Suite / platform" — Neon CRM + Neon Websites + Neon Giving + Neon Pay products. Formerly Kindful/NeonCRM.
**Content footprint:** Unknown; Cloudflare WAF hard-blocks programmatic access (403 to curl, WebFetch, Googlebot-UA). Audit relied on SERP + G2/Capterra/press.
**Pricing:** Tiered $99–$409/mo with % add-ons: +10% memberships, +20% events, 3% processing.
**Technical posture:** Aggressive WAF blocking (more restrictive than even DonorDock's old Cloudflare setup). Publishes "Generosity Report" + "Recurring Giving Report" — strong original research asset.
**DonorDock vs page:** Neon doesn't publish /vs/donordock. Their /compare covers Bloomerang/Salesforce/Blackbaud/DonorPerfect/WildApricot — not us.
**Threat:** Suite breadth + % markup model can look appealing to buyers who don't do TCO math.
**Attack surface:** "Suite vs ONE plan" is the cleanest wedge. $99 Essentials is a lure; real total with events + memberships + payments lands $150–$300+/mo before processing. DonorDock flat $500 wins clearly at 2,500+ contacts.

### Little Green Light (littlegreenlight.com) — Tier 3 🟢
**Position:** "Affordable yet powerful donor management for small and mid-sized nonprofits." 15+ years, 10,000+ customers, word-of-mouth growth, consultant ecosystem.
**Content footprint:** 502 blog posts + 136 static pages. Yoast/WordPress. Consistent weekly publishing.
**ICP overlap:** LGL's ICP skews smaller than DonorDock's upmarket direction. LGL wins the under-$1M-budget small nonprofit cohort. Decreasingly a DonorDock target as we move up.
**Pricing:** $45–$135/mo tiered by constituent count (up to 50k). No platform fees. Unlimited users.
**Technical posture:** Baseline Yoast schema (Organization/WebSite/BreadcrumbList). **No SoftwareApplication/Product/AggregateRating/FAQPage.** Publishes auto-generated llms.txt (Yoast-generated, thin). **Blocks ClaudeBot/CCBot/Google-Extended via Cloudflare** — opting out of AI search surface.
**DonorDock vs page:** LGL has ONE competitor page (Kindful alternative only). DonorDock's /compare/little-green-light-vs-donordock is live and uncontested.
**Threat:** Low. LGL is exiting the AI-search surface and under-invests in modern AEO/GEO. Their 10,000-customer switcher pool is a fixed source DonorDock should mine.
**Attack surface:** "Modern / AI / easy" keyword cluster. Migration-from-LGL hub. Unlimited-contacts angle (LGL jumps to $90/mo at 20k contacts; DonorDock flat).

### Keela (keela.co) — Tier 3 🟢
**Position:** "All-in-one nonprofit CRM and fundraising platform" with Keela Academy education layer. Canadian-origin with strong CRA/bilingual content.
**Content footprint:** 20K–35K ranking keywords (est); long-form education hub (3,000–10,000 word guides); templates + toolkits as lead magnets.
**ICP overlap:** High on small-mid US nonprofits. But Canadian/international vertical is their moat, not a DonorDock target.
**Pricing:** Tiered ~$99/mo starting, scales by contacts.
**Technical posture:** Webflow + Elementor + Yoast. Permissive AI bot posture. Article/BlogPosting + FAQPage schema on select pages.
**DonorDock vs page:** Keela does NOT publish /vs/donordock. First-mover wins the SERP.
**Threat:** Content volume + education positioning. Not a direct brand attacker.
**Attack surface:** US-specific IRS/501(c)(3)/state compliance content (Keela is weak here). "Keela alternatives for US nonprofits" SERP. Template/toolkit gap.

## Competitive themes — where DonorDock wins structurally

1. **Transparent pricing** ($500/mo flat + 1% online). Every Tier 1/2 competitor gates pricing or tiers by contact count.
2. **Unlimited contacts** — no contact-tier tax. Decisive at 2,500+ contacts vs Bloomerang, DonorPerfect, Neon.
3. **10 comparison pages** — unmatched. Bloomerang 0, DonorPerfect 5 (none vs DonorDock), NFG 0, Givebutter 1 (against DD), Neon 0, LGL 1, Keela 0, Virtuous 0.
4. **Strong homepage schema** — SoftwareApplication + AggregateRating + Offer. Beats Bloomerang, DonorPerfect, LGL on rich-result eligibility.
5. **Named founders + author bylines with Person schema** — Matt, Rob, Bridgette, Noah, Sami, Scott. DonorPerfect has 648 posts with ZERO bylines.
6. **Focused Fundraiser Podcast** (49+ episodes) — E-E-A-T asset no competitor matches.
7. **SOC 2 Type II** — surfaced in schema.
8. **Smart Stewardship as owned category** — now formalized; no competitor has a direct equivalent except Virtuous's "Responsive Fundraising."

## Competitive themes — where DonorDock trails

1. **Content volume:** 3–4x behind Bloomerang, Virtuous, DonorPerfect, LGL. Fixable via cluster strategy (pillar + spokes), not brute volume.
2. **Original research / benchmark reports:** Virtuous 5 reports, Neon "Generosity Report," Bloomerang retention benchmarks. DonorDock has $9B+ tracked gifts + 7,200 customers as raw material but no published report.
3. **Vertical landing pages:** LGL has 6 (animal care, human services, arts, land trusts, schools, libraries). DonorDock fewer.
4. **Glossary program:** DonorPerfect has 7 terms, LGL 0. DonorDock 0. High-volume AEO opportunity.
5. **Gated template/toolkit library:** Keela owns. DonorDock minimal.
6. **Named category term penetration:** Virtuous owns "Responsive Fundraising" externally; DonorDock's "Smart Stewardship" is seeded internally but not yet externally established.

## Rules for Phase 2 content drafting

When any Phase 2 article, battlecard, or page names a competitor:

1. **Cite only facts verified in the competitor audit files** — don't improvise. If the audit doesn't say it, don't write it.
2. **Update this landscape doc when Rob or the team learn new competitor intelligence** (e.g., pricing change, new product launch, acquisition).
3. **Quarterly refresh required** — re-verify pricing, content counts, technical posture at each quarter.
4. **Don't disparage** — accurate, factual, comparison-based language only.
5. **Always frame through Smart Stewardship** — we are not "just another nonprofit CRM." We are the Smart Stewardship platform.
