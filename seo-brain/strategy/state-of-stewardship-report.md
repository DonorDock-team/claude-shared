**Locked:** 2026-04-23
**Owner:** Rob Burke (CMO)
**Status:** Concept / scoping phase. Target publication Q4 2026.

## Why this over a generic fundraising benchmark

**Saturated space to avoid:**
- Bloomerang publishes annual retention benchmarks
- Neon One publishes "Generosity Report" (dollars-raised focused)
- Virtuous publishes vertical-specific fundraising benchmarks
- M+R, AFP, Giving USA, NonProfit PRO all publish fundraising dollar benchmarks
- Another generic "How much did nonprofits raise" report adds noise, not signal

**Uncontested space to own:**
- **Nobody has a stewardship-focused annual report**
- Stewardship is DonorDock's owned category (Smart Stewardship brand)
- Real GSC-verified search demand for stewardship content (125 imps for "best ai use cases for donor stewardship"; 105 for "how nonprofit crm supports donor stewardship at scale"; 97 for "donor stewardship cycle"; 58 for "donor stewardship platform comparison")
- No competitor publishes stewardship metrics publicly — zero category contenders
- Directly reinforces Smart Stewardship as the category DonorDock owns
- Aligns with Otto's AI narrative (AI as stewardship amplifier)

## Strategic goals

1. **Own "Smart Stewardship" as a category at industry level.** When a nonprofit leader Googles "donor stewardship report 2026" or asks ChatGPT "how often should I steward donors," DonorDock's data is the cited source.
2. **Build a citation asset AI engines preferentially return.** Primary research with named methodology + named authors + statistical rigor is what LLMs cite. None of the competitors have this in the stewardship slice.
3. **Generate backlinks + press.** Industry press (NonProfit PRO, The Chronicle of Philanthropy, Chronicle of Nonprofit Innovation) covers stewardship-angled data. Virtuous gets ~50 press mentions per RNS release; a well-framed stewardship report could earn comparable or better because it's a fresh angle.
4. **Feed Phase 2 content strategy.** Every pillar article can cite "According to DonorDock's 2026 State of Stewardship Report..." — perpetual internal citation fuel.
5. **Move ICP upmarket.** A rigorous research report speaks to development directors and EDs at growing/mid-sized nonprofits — not solo EDs reading listicles.

## Data DonorDock uniquely owns

From the ~1,300 customer nonprofits (7,200+ individual users) and $9B+ in tracked gifts, DonorDock can measure (anonymized and aggregated) what NO competitor can:

### Touchpoint frequency + consistency metrics

- **Median stewardship touchpoints per donor per year** across the customer base
- **Distribution:** what % of donors receive 0 touchpoints in a year? 1-3? 4-12? 13+?
- **Touchpoint mix by type:** email, text/SMS, handwritten/mail, phone call, in-person event, video message, social
- **Time-to-first-acknowledgment after gift** (median across customer base)
- **% of donors who receive a thank-you within 24 / 48 / 72 hours of their gift**

### Stewardship outcome metrics

- **Retention rate delta** between donors who receive >=4 stewardship touchpoints/year vs those who receive <=1 touchpoint
- **Second-gift rate** by stewardship-touchpoint frequency tier
- **Recurring-giving conversion rate** by stewardship quality band
- **Lifetime value (LTV) delta** by stewardship touchpoint consistency
- **Lapsed-donor reactivation rate** after proactive stewardship outreach vs no outreach

### Stewardship process / workflow metrics

- **% of donors with an assigned stewardship plan or journey**
- **Average #of days between scheduled stewardship touchpoints** (for assigned vs unassigned donors)
- **% of nonprofits using Smart Steward Method workflows** (Action Board adoption)
- **% of nonprofits using Otto AI-assisted stewardship** — and the retention delta for those nonprofits vs non-Otto users (the AI-stewardship proof point)
- **Tier-based stewardship frequency:** how do top customers steward major ($10K+) vs mid-level ($1K-$10K) vs recurring vs annual donors?

### Seasonal + Giving Tuesday / year-end patterns

- Stewardship touchpoint volume by month
- Year-end stewardship window behavior
- Giving Tuesday follow-up patterns

### Stewardship by nonprofit size

- How do stewardship touchpoint patterns differ for $1M-$3M nonprofits vs $3M-$8M vs $8M+?
- Does upmarket = better stewardship? Or worse (more donors, less time-per-donor)?

### Regional and vertical cuts (if statistically meaningful)

- Touchpoint frequency differences by vertical (human services vs arts vs faith-based vs environmental)
- Regional patterns (if meaningful) — US-only focus

## Methodology rigor requirements

To make this AI-citeable and press-worthy:

1. **Named sample size** — "Based on X donor-touchpoint events across ~1,300 nonprofits from Z time period"
2. **Normalization approach** — how did we handle skew, outliers, cohort differences
3. **Anonymization statement** — no individual nonprofit or donor identifiable
4. **Cross-organization aggregate only** — individual customer data never exposed
5. **Named primary author** — Matt + Rob + Noah (or combination)
6. **Named external reviewer** — CFRE-certified industry professional OR AFP affiliation OR named academic reviewer. This is critical for AI citation and press credibility.
7. **Public methodology doc** — separate methodology PDF for researchers
8. **Confidence intervals / statistical significance** called out on key findings
9. **Comparison to prior year** — even if 2026 is "year 1", reference publicly available 2024-2025 benchmarks from AFP / Giving USA for context
10. **Dataset schema on published data** — Dataset JSON-LD for Google Dataset Search indexing
