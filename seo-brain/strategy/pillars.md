## Cross-pillar content standards

1. **Every pillar page opens with Smart Stewardship framing** — not "all-in-one CRM" generic language.
2. **Every pillar page links to 15–25 supporting articles** via contextual in-body links (not just related-posts footer).
3. **Every pillar page has FAQPage schema with 10+ questions** (beat Bloomerang's 4-question baseline).
4. **Every supporting article links UP to its pillar and LATERALLY to 2–3 sibling pieces.**
5. **No pillar may target "small nonprofit," "first CRM," "solo ED," "church," or "tithing"** (per brand-positioning.md).
6. **Every article cites at least one original source or data point** (AFP, Giving USA, DonorDock customer aggregates, named expert).
7. **Every article has a named author with Person schema linked to a /team/* bio** (E-E-A-T for AI citation).
