before-after-chips
Compact "today vs after DonorDock" stack diagram for one-pagers. Strikethrough chips on the left for tools being replaced; solid purple chips on the right for new capabilities; an arrow between. Tools the prospect KEEPS (partner integrations) get a yellow `· kept` chip instead of being struck through.
competitor-comparison-table
Side-by-side dimension table comparing DonorDock to 2-3 competitors on dimensions like pricing model, migration cost, support level. DonorDock column visually distinguished in brand-purple.
cover-purple
Page 1 of a proposal or formal document. Establishes the brand voice, names the prospect, previews capabilities, and sets visual tone.
donor-intelligence-quad
The "AI + relationship intelligence" page. Shows how DonorDock helps the team know their donors and surface the right next steps automatically.
feature-checklist-grid
Compact grid of feature/capability labels for one-pagers. Shows platform breadth without taking up the full half-page that `platform-overview-quad` requires.
footer-band
Bottom-of-page meta line. Identifies the document context and provides the donordock.com URL or a page number.
header-band
Identifies the document and customer at the top of every interior page. DonorDock logo on the left, customer name in uppercase eyebrow style on the right, thin underline.
limited-time-offer-sidebar
Time-limited promotion callout. Used as a sidebar next to or under `pricing-card-purple` to flag a waived fee, partner discount, or signing deadline.
next-steps-numbered
Closing page of a proposal. 3-step numbered next-action list with a dark contact card at the bottom containing the rep's identity and a CTA button.
onboarding-3phase
Walks the prospect through the 90-day onboarding arc — Setup (1–30), Activation (31–60), Growth (61–90) — with concrete deliverables per phase. Reinforces that DonorDock includes white-glove migration.
platform-overview-quad
The "everything you get" page. A 4-quadrant grid covering the four pillars of the DonorDock platform: CRM / Outreach / Online Giving / Project Management. Each quadrant is sectional-color-coded.
pricing-card-purple
Investment summary page. Big purple price card up top, two-column breakdown (everything included + contextual sidebar) below, optional guarantee callout at the bottom.
quote-block
Standalone pull quote for anchoring a page with a customer or prospect voice. Standard purple-left-border treatment.
tool-consolidation
Before/after page that contrasts the prospect's current disconnected tools with DonorDock's consolidated capabilities. Two columns, equal weight, with a small red callout on the "before" side and a purple callout on the "after" side.
why-donordock-stats
The "why us" page. Stats row at the top, brand-story narrative on the left, G2 recognition + pull quote on the right.
your-moment
Frames the prospect's current situation against what they need from a platform. Sets up the rest of the proposal as "here's why this matters now."
