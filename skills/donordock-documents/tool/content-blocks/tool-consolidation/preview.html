Tool Consolidation

One platform
instead of four.

Your fundraising infrastructure is spread across disconnected tools. Data doesn't flow. Every supporter requires manual work in multiple places. DonorDock consolidates all of it.

Today: Four disconnected tools

Givebutter

Online donations — data stays in Givebutter, not connected to a CRM or donor record.

ThriveCart

Camper community investment plans — a workaround, not connected to donor stewardship.

Email Tool

Outreach goes out, but engagement data doesn't connect to giving or attendance records.

No CRM

No single record of who your donors, camper supporters, and participants actually are.

The cost of fragmentation

Manual re-entry. No holistic donor view. Cultivation by memory. Grant reporting requires piecing data from multiple sources.

With DonorDock: Everything connected

Giving Pages (replaces Givebutter)

Branded giving pages embedded on your website. All gifts flow directly into your donor records.

Camper Support Pages (replaces ThriveCart)

Unlimited individual pages with goals, tracking, and text-to-donate. Each gift tied to the specific camper.

Email + Text (replaces email tool)

Drag-and-drop email and two-way SMS built into the platform. Every open and click tracked on the contact record.

Donor CRM (new capability)

One complete profile per contact: giving history, attendance, engagement, notes, custom badges — all in one place.

The value of integration

Every gift, email, event, and camper sponsorship tracked to one record. Segment, report, and steward from one view.
